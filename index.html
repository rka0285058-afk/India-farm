Sources and assumptions
  • Workers: MoSPI Periodic Labour Force Survey, Annual Report 2025 (43.0% in farming, 44.8% in 2024; 61.6 crore workers).
  • Output: MoSPI National Accounts. The farm share is about 15–17% in the 2011-12 series and about 18% in the 2022-23 series. 16% is the figure in the question.
  • Land: Agriculture Census 2015-16 holdings and area by size class. Average holding 1.08 ha. The marginal-class area share (24%) comes from a NABARD working paper citing the census.
  • Income: NSS 77th round Situation Assessment of Agricultural Households (monthly income 8,337, of which wages 4,063 and leases 134).
  • Income by class assumes every hectare earns the same, calibrated so the average holding matches the survey income. Survey households and census holdings are different groups, so class incomes are indicative.
  • The bad-season loss, wage pass-through and mover pay are scenario inputs, not data. The gain from moving workers is an upper-bound illustration.
  • The land Gini counts only differences between size classes, so it understates true inequality.
